Buying it ready-made

All three shops sell the same RS box. RS means the car comes with its remote, but without a battery and charger. I found no shop in the EU that sells the box with a battery (RTR).

What is in the RS box

  • The car, fully built, with body and lights (head, turn, reverse, roof)
  • FMS MG6 remote, 6 channels (needs 4 AA batteries, not included)
  • 550 32T motor, 2-speed gearbox, 25 kg steering servo
  • Roof rack, jerry cans, sand ladders, snorkel, brush guard
  • Interior with a driver figure, small tools, manual
  • Not in the box: LiPo battery, charger, AA batteries

The AI summary on the AliExpress page says "batteries included". That is wrong. The seller's own spec sheet says "RS, battery not included".

The real AliExpress price

The car costs more than €150, so the EU's low-price import rule does not cover it. You still pay 4.7% customs duty (EU code 9503 00 75 00), and VAT on top of that duty.

Check before you buy

Wheelbase is shorterEvery Discovery body I found fits a 313 mm chassis. The FMS is 321 mm, so your car will be 8 mm shorter.
Ask sellers two questionsDo the axles have locked diffs? Does the 2-speed gearbox come with a spur gear and motor plate? The listings do not say.
Some prices are from search pagesAliExpress started showing a robot check, so the body, battery, charger, LEDs and small parts have search prices. These can be for a cheaper option of the item.
Sales end on 17 OctoberMany AliExpress prices are sale prices. Expect them to change.
LiPo batteries and shippingAliExpress may not ship LiPo batteries to Latvia. An EU shop is safer. Also check the battery fits the battery tray.
Not countedPaint for the body, hex drivers and other tools, a soldering iron, and shock oil when you buy a kit.
Customs on small parcelsSince July 2026 the EU charges about €3 duty on each low-value parcel. Each AliExpress seller usually sends its own parcel. This page counts one parcel for each part.
No 32T motor foundThe closest cheap motor is 35T. It is a bit slower and a bit stronger than the FMS motor.
